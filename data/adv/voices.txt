# 潮鳴りの密室 — speech blips (built by tools/adv/build.py with tools/adv/voice.py into the SPC700 driver)
#
# While the body of a spoken page is typed ("NAME> text" lines: the six people and わたし), a short
# round blip sounds on voice 1 of the driver, at most once every "gap" frames, on drawn voiced glyphs
# only.  No sound for the narration, the yellow names, notebook/hint/system headings, menus, the
# characters of "silent:", during the pause after punctuation (the mouth's GlyphPause), in page / end
# waits, and with the sound off.  Every blip is an original waveform computed here (no recorded sound).
#
#   @timbre NAME            harmonics: amplitude of partial 1, 2, 3, ... (sine partials)
#   @voice SPEAKER          a person's name of the script, or わたし
#     timbre:   timbre name
#     pitch:    fundamental at the start of the blip, Hz
#     glide:    pitch change over the blip, % (negative: falls, the round "po")
#     length:   blip length, ms (attack + decay to silence)
#     attack:   rise time, ms (raised cosine; the decay is a raised cosine too)
#     volume:   DSP voice volume 0-127 (left = right; set over the songs, which duck 10 dB under messages)
#     gap:      frames from one blip to the next at the least (2 frames = one glyph at normal speed)
#   silent:     characters that never start a blip (punctuation, brackets, marks, the space)
#   contour:    8 pitch offsets in % for the blips of a phrase (1st .. 8th; then the 5th-8th repeat);
#               a phrase starts on a page and after every pause

silent: 、。，．・：；？！ー…‥〜「」『』（）［］〔〕　―－
contour: 2.0 0.5 1.0 0.0 0.5 -1.0 0.0 -1.5

@timbre round
harmonics: 1.0 0.32 0.10 0.04

@timbre dark
harmonics: 1.0 0.22 0.05

@timbre soft
harmonics: 1.0 0.45 0.20 0.08 0.03
