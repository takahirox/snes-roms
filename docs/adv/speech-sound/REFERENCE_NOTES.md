# Reference research on speech sounds

2026-10-30. Speech sounds in commercial games were researched as short voice-like sounds that accompany text display. No ROMs, samples or sound effects from the referenced works are brought into this game.

The model in this environment does not support audio input. What follows is based on frames of public videos that were actually retrieved and on waveforms and spectrograms of their audio data, not on evaluation by listening. Because the audio mixes background music and sound effects, no claim is made to have measured the exact pitch or length of individual speech sounds.

| Source | Scope and method of checking | Observations and use for the design |
|---|---|---|
| [ファミコン探偵倶楽部 PART II うしろに立つ少女 (Famicom Detective Club Part II: The Girl Who Stands Behind), SFC gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxS_nu34Ozc) — official Nintendo channel | A 46-second official video showing the SFC game screen inside a Wii U Virtual Console introduction frame. Dialogue text display checked frame by frame. Audio from 19–27 s examined with waveforms and FFT. | Short sound pulses cluster while text is displayed and break off in between. Used as a reference for not sustaining sounds for long and for creating groupings and rests that follow the text display. |
| [EarthBound SNES Walkthrough Longplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3JfATnXbqg) — PlayingWithMahWii, no commentary | Of a video over 9 hours long, only 6:00–6:30 was retrieved. Movement, NPC dialogue and scene fades checked. 12–19 s of the retrieved segment compared with waveforms and FFT. | Groups of short, sharp pulses and rests are visible during dialogue display. Movement and fade sounds are not counted as speech sounds. Reference for a design that generates sound only while text is appearing and leaves nothing during waits. |
| [逆転裁判123 成歩堂セレクション (Ace Attorney Trilogy) promotional video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UW6RfwjnJgI) — CapcomChannel | Of a 106-second official video, only 0:10–0:40 was retrieved. The early part has character text display; the later part has promotional captions. Audio from 0:10–0:18 examined with waveforms and FFT. Treated as an introduction video for the HD version, not a recording of the GBA version. | Dialogue text and promotional captions were distinguished. Because the audio also contains music and staging sounds, the timbre and interval of the pure text sound are not asserted. |
| [『逆転裁判6』 (Ace Attorney: Spirit of Justice) developer interview: how the characters were born](https://www.ndw.jp/post-2863/) — Nintendo DREAM WEB | Checked the developer's remarks in the section on Pot-Dino. It explains that the text sound was used for a singing effect and that an instrument motif was conveyed to the sound production. | A first-hand reference from the production side that even short speech sounds can carry character. In this game this becomes modest differences in pitch, harmonics and length. The exact implementation of ordinary speech sounds is not inferred from this article. |

The twenty-odd ms sound, the minimum interval of about 4 frames, the volume and so on adopted in this game are original design values fitted to this game's text speed and the screen of a calm mystery adventure, not values extracted from the works above. The waveforms are newly made from sine harmonics, a short attack and decay and a slight pitch change, converted to BRR and played on the SPC700.

The reference videos, WAVs and cropped images were kept in local temporary files for the research and are not included in the distributed ROM, the candidate's source set or the public review videos. The retrieval tool was the official yt-dlp 2026.10.26, with its SHA-256 verified. Audio analysis used 16 kHz, FFT 256, and waveforms and spectrograms in 5 ms steps. Checking naturalness by ear remains a separate evaluation.
